## 4. Sample locks that later agents keep breaking / 反复会写错的样本

- Store **ABE**, not SBE.
- The bottom printed 氧合指数 on an **arterial** sheet is P/F. A blank middle 氧合指数 is not 0. A percent row such as 0.28% is not P/F. A blank A-a row is not 0 and must not be filled with ctO2.

- Stated MAP is stored as stated. Do not replace it with `(SBP+2×DBP)/3`.
- Norepinephrine only at **0.05 mg/mL**. Do not invent dopamine mg/h or weight.
- A number returning inside range is not “weaned”, “infection cured”, “bleeding stopped”, “albumin already given”, “feeding restored”, or “kidneys recovered”.
